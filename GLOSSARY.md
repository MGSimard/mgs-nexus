# MGS Nexus

The single public website at mgsimard.dev. It holds MGSimard's portfolio, working stack, saved references and notes, and shows how that workspace operates.

## Language

### Site

**Nexus**:
The root website served at mgsimard.dev, and the only place its content lives.
_Avoid_: main site, hub, portfolio site

**Subdomain App**:
A standalone application served on a subdomain of mgsimard.dev, built and versioned separately from the Nexus.
_Avoid_: sub-app, side project, satellite

### Sections

**Work**:
The section of the Nexus showcasing finished or ongoing projects, across web and game development.
_Avoid_: Projects, Portfolio, Webdev, Gamedev

**Stack**:
The section listing the tools, services and agent tooling currently in active use.
_Avoid_: Loadout, Recs, Recommendations, Uses

**Intel**:
The section collecting external material saved for reference, such as articles, tweets, tools, UI libraries and design references. It is the only place saved material lives.
_Avoid_: Library, Utility, Bookmarks, References, Notes, Field Notes

**Schema**:
The public section where the Nexus design system and UI library components are viewed and explored.
_Avoid_: Storybook, Playground, Design Space, Components

**Logs** _(reserved, not yet built)_:
The section that will publish **Logs**.
_Avoid_: Blog, Transmissions

### Design System

**Variant**:
A design choice fixed when a component is authored, such as its intent or size.
_Avoid_: Style, flavour, type, mode

**State**:
An interaction condition a component passes through at runtime, such as disabled, open, checked or invalid.
_Avoid_: Status, variant

**Specimen**:
One rendering of a component in **Schema**, showing a single combination of **Variants** in a single **State**.
_Avoid_: Story, example, preview, demo

### Content

**Log** _(reserved, not yet built)_:
A finished, dated, long-form piece written for an audience.
_Avoid_: post, blog post, Transmission, article (an **Intel** article is someone else's writing that was saved)

**Snapshot**:
A local copy of an external item's content and media, captured when the item is added, so the entry survives if the original is deleted or changed.
_Avoid_: cache, mirror, embed
